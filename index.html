THE FOUR PHASES OF AI VALUE

Follow the value
beyond usage.

A framework I use to ask better questions about AI adoption and what it makes possible.

  1. 01

    Activity

    Are people using it?

    Understand usage, workflow fit, and the habits taking shape.

  2. 02

    Efficiency

    Is the work getting better?

    Measure time, quality, and effort against a clear baseline.

  3. 03

    Capacity Redeployed

    What happens to the time returned?

    Work with leaders to put capacity toward higher-value work.

  4. 04

    Business Impact

    What changed for the business?

    Connect the change in work to outcomes the organization values.

Time returned creates an opportunity. Business impact depends on what happens next.
