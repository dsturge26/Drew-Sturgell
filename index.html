GLOBAL SOFTWARE DE-RISK / ESTABLISHED PROGRAM

Create a renewal program that lasts.

Processes reached approximately 25,000 sellers and customer success professionals and remain in use more than four years after launch and subsequent operational handoff.

What I owned: Program design, senior sponsorship, and coordination across teams. I supplied business context to the engineers building the AI/ML risk model.

Observed outcomes and context +

Across four years, targeted high-risk accounts renewed in the 70% range versus the 20% range for eligible accounts not targeted.

Both groups were high risk, but higher-ARR accounts received priority. This is an observed cohort comparison, not a controlled measure of the program’s effect. Approximate rates reported by Drew in September 2026. This program is separate from the newer messaging POC.
